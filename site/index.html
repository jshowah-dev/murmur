Your voice never leaves your computer

Speech recognition runs on your computer's processor, using NVIDIA's Parakeet model. After the one-time download, Murmur works with Wi-Fi off. Your audio is processed in memory and never saved or sent anywhere.

And the small things

  • Hands-freeDouble-tap the key to keep listening without holding it. Tap again to finish.
  • Fix the last oneHold Shift with the key to edit what it just typed. It learns from the fix.
  • Spoken commandsSay “new line”, “new paragraph” or “question mark” when you want them.
  • SnippetsSay a trigger phrase and a saved block of text goes in instead.
  • HistoryYour last 25 dictations, a click away in the tray or menu bar.
  • Quiet while you talkYour speakers mute while the key is held.

Download

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, x64. No GPU needed.

Get the installer

Installs for your user only, no admin prompt, and updates itself from the tray. Not code-signed yet: if SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway.

macOS

macOS 13 or later, Apple Silicon (M1 or newer).

Get the DMG

Signed and notarized by Apple. Lives in the menu bar, with its pill hanging from the notch, and updates itself from there.
