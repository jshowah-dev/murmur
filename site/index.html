What you say, and what lands

Pick up milk and coffee on the way home.

Fillers go, punctuation and capitals come in.

Teach it names and jargon once. It matches sound-alikes too, and learns from your fixes.

You said, in Outlook

hi sarah thanks for the update i'll send notes by friday thanks jeff

Murmur typed

Hi Sarah, Thanks for the update. I'll send notes by Friday. Thanks, Jeff

Speech recognition runs on your computer's processor, using NVIDIA's Parakeet model. After the one-time download, Murmur works with Wi-Fi off. Your audio is processed in memory and never saved or sent anywhere.

Installs for your user only, no admin prompt, and updates itself from the tray. Not code-signed yet: if SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway.
