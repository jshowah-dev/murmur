What it does

  1. 01

    Nothing leaves your machine

    Speech recognition runs on your CPU with a local model. No account, no cloud, no network after the one-time model download.

  2. 02

    Types where your cursor is

    Any app that accepts typing. Hands-free by double-tap, spoken commands like “new line”, filler words dropped, last 25 dictations in the tray.

  3. 03

    Learns your words

    A dictionary for names and jargon with phonetic matching, snippets for saved text, and “fix last” teaches it from your corrections.

Two minutes, start to finish

Get the DMG

Not signed by Apple yet. On first launch open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Needed once per version.
